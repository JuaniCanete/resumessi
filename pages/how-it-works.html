❓ How It Works

Complete workflow: setup → generation → evaluation → export

1. Generate or Load Resume

Use Generate with AI button in left panel to create a resume from prompt data, or load existing resume data from JSON.

2. Paste Job Description

Copy any job posting and paste it into the left panel.

3. ATS Scan

Click ATS Score Check to send resume + job description to AI for evaluation.

4. Review Results

Check the right panel for score, breakdown, feedback, and missing keywords.

5. Polish & Export

Refine your resume with AI or download as PDF directly.
